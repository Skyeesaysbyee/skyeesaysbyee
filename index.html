01 / THE DRIVER

HEY, I'M SKYEE.

I'm a car enthusiast and creator from the Portland area. I love old Japanese cars, building things with my hands, learning new skills, and documenting the process along the way.

My main project is a 1987 Toyota MR2 AW11. What started as an old sports car has become a much bigger adventure involving restoration, fabrication, friends, and a lot of unexpected repairs.

I'm also working toward IKOU, my vision for an automotive community and future original aero brand. For now, I'm just enjoying the journey.
