ABOUT ME.

Cars, creative projects, and the occasional mystery noise from a 39-year-old Toyota.

HEY, I'M SKYEE.

I'm a car enthusiast and creator from the Portland area. I love old Japanese cars, learning how things work, building things with my hands, and sharing the process along the way.

My main project is a 1987 Toyota MR2 AW11. It started as an old sports car and quickly became an adventure in repairs, restoration, fabrication, and meeting other people who love cars.

I'm documenting the wins, the mistakes, and everything in between. There's a lot more to come.
